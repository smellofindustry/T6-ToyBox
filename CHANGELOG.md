# Changelog

## 1.0.82

- **Eye colors** color the eyeball too, not just the glow.
- **Friend page:** it opens on a home screen with the match, who and when. Sections open one at a time, and a bar at the bottom shows what you sent and what the game said.
- **Quick** is ten small buttons, starting with the ones this phone used last. Do again is now part of Quick.
- Share in Host controls shows the code when the browser won't copy it.
- **New defaults for friends:** nearly every button works right away, while skipping rounds, Kill All, God Mode, eye color and random events stay the host's, and the rough buttons are spaced out (Take Gun once a round, Take Perks every 3 rounds, spawns and power-ups every 20 to 30 seconds for each friend). If you never changed your settings, you get them too. **Use the defaults** in Host controls puts them back any time.
- A friend's Quick buttons skip the ones the host turned off.
- The roulette wheel's clicks are louder.
- The Panic button is gone from the teddy bear menu and Host controls.
- Fake-A-Friend shows in the friend list at the top right while it's on, with a white square.
- The TOY BOX settings tab: **Friend controller** is gone (pause friends in Host controls), and it now says **Flash link on screen**, **Fake-A-Friend** and **Mod compatibility mode**.
- Mob of the Dead: asking for Brutus again while he's still on his way no longer sends a second one.
- **Game icons:** more of the friend page and Host controls use the game's own art: the round in real chalk marks, the Mystery Box for roulette, the teddy bear for Move the box, Pack-a-Punch, the Insta-Kill icon, after-match report icons for doors, bounties and zombies, and the hellhound, Brutus and the Panzer in Spawn enemies.
- **Setups:** save how you like Host controls under a name, load it again in one click, and share it with friends as a code.
- Host controls has an amber bar across the top, and the friend page now says Friend remote, so it's easy to tell which page you're on.
- **Roulette:** friends spin a wheel of good and bad effects. It spins on everyone's screen, slows down, lands on one, and that happens.
- The version shows next to the name: in the Mods menu (Toy Box v1.0.34), in Host controls and on the friend page. The last number goes up with every build.
- **Friend colors:** each friend gets an eye color when they join, and the zombies they send have it. Who's on the friend link shows at the top right of the screen, each with their color.
- Changing everyone's eye color is the host's by default. Turn **Eye color** on in Host controls to let friends do it.
- **Bounty prizes and penalties:** a bounty can give a good effect when it's won (Max Ammo, a free perk, a good spin...) and a bad one when it's lost (Take Gun, sprinters, the map's boss, a bad spin...).
- **Countdowns:** effects that last a while show on screen, top right, with the time left. Your own (like infinite ammo) only show on your screen.
- **The screen:** an 8-bit TV on the friend page and in Host controls, off until you turn it on. It plays little scenes from the match: hordes mowed down, bounties paid with a shot through the glass, goodies on a parachute, enemies, tricks, downs, roulette and new rounds, with each player as their map's character. In between, the players walk around and shoot the odd zombie. Sound is optional.

## Alpha 1.0 (1.0.0)

The first public build.

- **Friend link:** friends open a link, pick a name and get buttons for power-ups, perks, weapons, enemies, points, rounds, the map, tricks, scares and bounties.
- **Playing alone:** the game plays a friend for you, fair or troll, at chill, normal or wild.
- **ToyBox.exe:** one-click install, the teddy bear by the clock with the friend link, and Host controls.
- **Limits:** the host can put a cooldown (seconds or rounds) or a number of uses per match on any button, group or every command.
- **Match stats** for contests, as CSV or JSON.
- **Random events** every few rounds.
- **Zombie eye colors:** 13 colors, or any hex color (it takes the closest), and white eyes on zombies friends send.
- **Console commands** for everything (`set tb help`), and an API for other mods.
- Every zombies map, Survival and Classic.
