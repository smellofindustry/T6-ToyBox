# Playing alone

No friends around? The game can play one for you.

1. In a Toy Box match, press **Esc**.
2. Go to **Options > Settings > TOY BOX**.
3. Set **Simulate a friend** to **FAIR** or **TROLL**.
4. Set **Intensity** to **CHILL**, **NORMAL** or **WILD**.
5. Go back to the game.

A few seconds later, "*gamertag* joined the controller" pops up.

## Fair

Fair watches how you're doing.

- Quiet for a while? They push back: a horde, sprinters, hellhounds or Jumping Jacks.
- Tougher zombies come from round 8. Brutus or a Panzer from round 12.
- After each push, you get time to breathe.
- Struggling? They help: a Max Ammo, an Insta-Kill, a short freeze.
- They never take anything away.

## Troll

Troll messes with you about once a minute. More on wild, less on chill.

- A Max Ammo that turns out to be fake (then a real one)
- A free perk you lose if you don't get the kills in time
- A stampede of sprinters, with a speed boost for you
- 500 points taken, then 1000 given back
- Low gravity, dares, hellhounds, a moved box, lights out

## Good to know

- They sit out while a real friend is pressing buttons, and come back a minute later.
- They show up in [match stats](match-stats.md) under their gamertag.
- You can also switch them in [Host controls](host-controls.md).
- Or in the console: `set tb simulate fair`, `set tb simulate troll wild`, `set tb simulate off`. `set tb simulate now` makes them act right away.
