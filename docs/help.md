# Something not working?

## No link on screen

1. Check the teddy bear is by the clock. If it isn't, open ToyBox.exe again and click **Reinstall**.
2. Check you picked **Mods > Toy Box** in the game.
3. Click the teddy bear > **Show link in game**.

## The link won't open

- Send the newest link. It changes every time the game restarts.
- A brand-new link can take about 20 seconds to start working.

## Friends' buttons do nothing

- Your game might be paused. A solo game pauses in menus and when you alt-tab.
- Or friends are paused in the teddy bear menu.
- Their page tells them which.

## Still stuck?

1. Click the teddy bear > **Open log**.
2. [Open a bug report](https://github.com/smellofindustry/T6-ToyBox/issues) with the map and what happened.
3. Attach the log.

---

## Update

Download the new ToyBox.exe and open it.

Your link, settings and match history stay.

## Uninstall

Windows **Settings > Apps > Installed apps > Toy Box > Uninstall**.
